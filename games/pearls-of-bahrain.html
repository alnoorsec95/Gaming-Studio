Catch & Dodge

Pearls of Bahrain · لؤلؤ البحرين

Sail your dhow along the coast. Catch the falling pearls (golden ones are worth 5, rare fast deep purple ones are worth 10) and dodge the jellyfish. The whole scene changes every 25 pearls. Easy mode: 5 lives, and you are safe for a moment after a sting. Drag or use the arrow keys to steer.
